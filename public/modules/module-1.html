Platform Selection Mastery

Most flippers default to GoDaddy Auctions for everything. That's a mistake. Each platform has a completely different buyer pool — and listing on the wrong one means your domain is invisible to the buyers who would actually pay for it.

01

GoDaddy Auctions

Best for high-traffic generic keywords, .com domains with search volume, and domains priced under $1,000. Largest buyer pool but most competitive listing environment. Default choice for volume-moving.

02

NameJet

Best for exact-match keyword domains, expired domains with backlink profiles, and premium .com and .net domains over $500. Attracts serious domain investors with real acquisition budgets.

03

Sedo

Best for high-value brandables, international domains, and premium domains over $2,000. Strong European buyer base. Also the best platform for outbound negotiation via direct messaging.

04

Dan.com

Best for geo domains, local business names, and niche-specific names in the $500-$5,000 range. Lowest buyer friction — fast checkout reduces drop-off on willing buyers.

05

The Decision Framework

Step 1: Classify your domain type. Step 2: Determine price tier. Step 3: Match to platform. Step 4: List on ONE platform only — splitting inventory dilutes bidder attention and creates confusion.

Key Takeaways

→Never list the same domain on multiple platforms simultaneously
→Dan.com is consistently underused for geo and local business domains
→NameJet's buyer pool skews toward serious investors — better for higher reserves
→Check Sedo for international interest before setting reserve on premium brandables
