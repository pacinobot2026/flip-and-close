Auction Listing Copywriting

Buyers do not buy domain names. They buy what the domain represents for their business. Your listing title and description are your only opportunity to communicate that vision before they click away. This module teaches the 7-element formula that wins auctions.

01

Element 1 — The Business Vision Title

Describe the business someone could build, not just the domain. "DenverPlumber.com — Perfect for Denver-Based Plumbing Services" beats "DenverPlumber.com" every time.

02

Element 2 — Category and Use Case

State explicitly what type of business this domain is built for. Buyers search by category. Being specific increases relevance ranking and buyer confidence.

03

Element 3 — Keyword and SEO Value

If the domain has keyword search volume, state the monthly search figure. This is concrete ROI a buyer can calculate.

04

Element 4 — Comparable Sales Reference

Include one comparable sale: "Similar domains like [X] sold for $[Y] in [year]." This anchors your price against real market data.

05

Elements 5-7 — Urgency, Extension Justification, Outreach CTA

Reinforce the auction deadline. Justify non-.com extensions with a business rationale. Include an outreach invitation: "Interested before auction closes? Contact via platform messaging."

Key Takeaways

→The title determines 80% of whether a buyer reads further or scrolls past
→Never use placeholder descriptions — they signal you do not believe in your own domain
→The comparable sales reference adds significant credibility when available
