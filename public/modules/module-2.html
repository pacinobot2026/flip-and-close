The Reserve Price Formula

Setting the wrong reserve price is the number one reason domain auctions fail. Too high and nobody bids. Too low and you leave money on the table. This module gives you a data-backed calculation framework for every major domain type.

01

Step 1 — Find Comparables

Go to NameBio.com and search for domains similar to yours that have sold. Filter by extension, character length, and keyword type. Pull 5-10 comparable sales from the last 24 months.

02

Step 2 — Calculate the Median

Remove the highest and lowest outlier from your comparables. Calculate the median of the remaining sales. This is your baseline market value.

03

Step 3 — Apply Domain Multipliers

.com = 1.0x baseline. .net = 0.3x. .org = 0.25x. Exact keyword match = +20%. Geo domain = +15%. Under 8 characters = +25%. Expired with backlinks = +30-50%.

04

Step 4 — Set Reserve and Buy-It-Now

Reserve = 60% of calculated value. Buy-It-Now = 140% of calculated value. This spread creates urgency while protecting your floor.

05

Use the Pricing Calculator

The interactive Domain Pricing Calculator in your member area runs this formula automatically. Enter your metrics and get an instant price range.

Key Takeaways

→NameBio is your most important research tool — use it before every listing
→Always offer a Buy-It-Now price — serious buyers often skip auctions entirely
→Under-pricing signals low quality to experienced buyers — both directions matter
→Aged domains with clean backlink profiles routinely sell for 3-5x comparable fresh domains
