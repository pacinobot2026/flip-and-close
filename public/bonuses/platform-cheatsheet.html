Platform Comparison Cheat Sheet

All four major domain auction platforms compared side by side. See Module 1 for the full platform selection framework.

GoDaddy Auctions

15% success fee
Listing Fee

$5 listing fee

Best For

Exact-match .com, high search volume, under $1,000

Avoid For

Premium brandables, high-value domains over $5k

Buyer Profile

Retail buyers, small business owners, beginner investors

Pro tip: Largest volume but most competition. Best for moving inventory quickly at fair market.

NameJet

10% success fee
Listing Fee

No listing fee (partner only domains)

Best For

Expired domains with backlinks, premium .com and .net, $500+

Avoid For

Low-value domains under $200, obscure extensions

Buyer Profile

Serious domain investors, portfolio builders, SEO agencies

Pro tip: Pre-registrations create built-in competition before auction even starts. List high-value expired domains here.

Sedo

10-15% success fee
Listing Fee

No listing fee

Best For

Premium brandables, international domains, $2,000+, outbound negotiations

Avoid For

Generic low-value domains, under $500 asks

Buyer Profile

International buyers (strong EU base), corporate acquirers, brand builders

Pro tip: Best platform for outbound negotiation on premium domains. Messaging system allows direct buyer contact.

Dan.com

9% success fee
Listing Fee

No listing fee

Best For

Geo domains, local business names, $500-$5,000, fast-checkout buyers

Avoid For

Very high-value domains over $10k, obscure niches

Buyer Profile

Local business owners, entrepreneurs, digital agencies

Pro tip: Lowest buyer friction. Checkout is fast and simple — reduces drop-off on willing buyers. Best for geo and niche domains.
